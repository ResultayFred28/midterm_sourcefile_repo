Working through this array exercise finally made these methods click for me, instead of just
feeling like a vocabulary list I had to memorize.

Part C was definitely the toughest. I was chaining shift, pop, and splice, and I quickly
realized I couldn't just assume a student's index would stay the same. I had to slow down and 
track how the array was changing in real-time. Figuring out that I needed to use indexOf() 
right before splice()—rather than relying on an old index—was the "aha" moment that 
kept my studentNames and studentScores perfectly synced.

Part E also tripped me up at first. I ran filter() and map() on the studentScores array 
and expected the results to be the exact same length. When passingScores came back 
with four items and passFailLabels had five, I honestly thought my code was broken.
But it forced me to realize the core difference: filter cuts out items that don't match a condition, 
while map transforms every single item, keeping the original length intact. I definitely won't forget that distinction now.

If I take one big thing away from this, it's how careful you have to be when adding or removing 
items from parallel arrays to keep them aligned. It also completely solidified the difference 
between methods that mutate the original array and those that spit out a brand new one.
